# ADR-006: Material conclusions require provenance

## Context

GTM labels such as fit, timing, or intent are unsafe when their supporting observations cannot be
inspected.

## Decision

Material conclusions must retain source, observation time, evidence reference, interpreting
version, reason code, and probabilistic confidence where applicable.

## Consequences

Unsupported facts are rejected or marked as hypotheses. UI decision traces can explain why a
conclusion exists.

## Status

Accepted
