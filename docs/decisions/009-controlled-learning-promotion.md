# ADR-009: Learning changes require promotion

## Context

Outcome-driven improvement is valuable, but autonomous production policy or strategy rewrites are
unsafe and difficult to audit.

## Decision

Learning produces versioned change proposals that require evaluation, approval, and explicit
promotion.

## Consequences

Historical versions remain reproducible. No process silently rewrites ICP, thresholds, messaging,
offer, pricing, or policy.

## Status

Accepted
